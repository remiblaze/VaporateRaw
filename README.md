# Vaporate Raw: one-knob spectral wash

![Vaporate Raw free one-knob spectral wash UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/VaporateRaw/main/vaporateraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One VAPOR knob turns any sound into a lush, moving spectral wash.**

Vaporate Raw is the free, one-knob version of Vaporate. Feed it audio, turn up VAPOR, and its FFT spectral engine dissolves your signal into an ambient smear of mist and air, great on vocals, pads, synths, and FX returns.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/VaporateRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`VaporateRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Vaporate Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`VaporateRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Vaporate Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **VAPOR** knob. Turn it up and a real FFT (STFT) spectral engine smears your sound into an ambient, ever-moving wash. The macro drives smear, blur, stereo spread, feedback, and a gentle darkening tilt all at once. No menus, no setup: just feed it audio and vaporize.

## 💻 System Requirements

**macOS**

**Windows**
- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- A VST3 or CLAP host
