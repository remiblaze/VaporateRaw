# Vaporate Raw — one-knob spectral wash

![Vaporate Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/VaporateRaw/main/vaporateraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One VAPOR knob turns any sound into a lush, moving spectral wash.**

Vaporate Raw is the free, one-knob version of Vaporate. Feed it audio, turn up VAPOR, and its FFT spectral engine dissolves your signal into an ambient smear of mist and air — great on vocals, pads, synths, and FX returns.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/VaporateRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`VaporateRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **VAPOR** knob. Turn it up and a real FFT (STFT) spectral engine smears your sound into an ambient, ever-moving wash — the macro drives smear, blur, stereo spread, feedback, and a gentle darkening tilt all at once. No menus, no setup: just feed it audio and vaporize.
